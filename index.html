Document tools in development

▤

Capture and organize

Import photos and PDFs, keep the originals, and detect duplicate files.

⌕

Search your vault

Search by keyword or meaning and open the original document from a result.

≡

Review financial details

Check suggested dates, issuers, totals, and tax amounts before including them in confirmed reports.

✦

Ask your documents

Find answers in selected excerpts and inspect the supporting sources.

◎

Multilingual interface

Use the app in English, German, or Persian, with right-to-left layout for Persian.

↗

Optional cloud AI

Potential provider connections, including ChatGPT-based analysis, are under evaluation. These integrations are unavailable to the public during private development.

◇

Privacy and review

  • Encrypted storage on your device
  • Offline access to core vault features
  • Explicit review before financial confirmation
  • User approval before sending document content to cloud AI

Document extraction can miss details or misread a value. The app presents suggested fields for review so you can check what came from the original document.
